**2. recommend.py exports** — `__all__` (recommend.py:311-317): `Suggestion`, `NEED_WEIGHT`, `SCARCITY_WEIGHT`, `recommend`, `recommend_need_aware`. **Private**: `_category_gaps` (recommend.py:161-192) returns `(gaps, spreads)` per cat: `gap = mine − league_median` in better direction (`DIRECTIONS`), normalized by league spread (0→1), clamped ≥0; `spread` raw. Recommendation: **promote `_category_gaps`** (drop underscore, add to `__all__`) — P3's per-cat BUILD/COAST/PUNT tags are literally gap thresholds on the same normalized gap; reuse avoids duplicating the median/spread/direction math and the documented zero-inflation caveat (recommend.py:186-191).
